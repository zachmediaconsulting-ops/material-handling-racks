Rack Repair

Material handling racks take abuse.

Forklift impacts, damaged welds, bent frames, worn locating points, broken components, and years of repeated use can eventually make an otherwise valuable rack unsafe or unusable.

Instead of automatically replacing existing racks, we can inspect, repair, reinforce, and restore them for continued use.

Learn More

Rack Changeovers & ReWorks

Manufacturing programs change. Your racks should be able to change with them.

When a component, vehicle platform, assembly process, automation cell, or packaging requirement changes, existing racks may no longer properly support the new application.

Rather than scrapping an entire fleet of material handling racks, we can modify and rework existing racks for a new part or program.
