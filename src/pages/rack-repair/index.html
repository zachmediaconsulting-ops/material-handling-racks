Michigan Steel Rack Repair Services

Rivers provides professional rack repair services for automotive, assembly, and industrial material handling applications. We repair damaged steel racks, shipping racks, returnable racks, and custom material handling racks to restore strength, function, and service life.

From cracked welds and damaged structural components to worn dunnage and other high-use areas, our team has the fabrication experience to bring existing racks back into working condition.
